4 · Limitations and required experimental work

Not assessed by anything here:

  • Intrinsic thiol reactivity (kGSH) — the primary safety determinant for a covalent inhibitor. No in-silico surrogate.
  • Cysteine-proteome selectivity — off-target covalent modification. Requires chemoproteomics (e.g. isoTOP-ABPP).
  • Reactive-metabolite formation — GSH-adduct trapping in microsomes; not predicted here.
  • Time-dependent CYP inhibition — distinct from the reversible inhibition predicted above, and more consequential.
  • ICH M7 second methodology — an expert rule-based system (Derek/Leadscope) plus expert review of every positive and out-of-domain call.
